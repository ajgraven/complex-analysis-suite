Hele-Shaw Flow

Free-boundary flow of a viscous fluid in a Hele-Shaw cell — the moving boundary as a conformal map of the unit disk, evolving in time. Two facets of the same physics.

Exact · closed form

Twisting quadrature domain

The Graven–Makarov one-point unbounded quadrature domain, driven by a complex charge α = q + iγ: it grows and twists up to a double point or a (3,2)-cusp. Every quantity is closed form; the injected charge is conserved and shown live. Drivable by a one-point QD authored in the Quadrature Domains app.

Numerical · ≈

Interior-droplet evolver

A bounded droplet grown from a central source by numerically integrating the classical Polubarinova–Galin equation. Injection smooths a perturbed circle; suction is ill-posed and fingers into a cusp. The conserved Richardson moments ride along as the honest error bar.

One of the Complex Analysis Suite apps. Locally: pnpm --filter hele-shaw-flow dev.
