Complex Analysis Suite

A suite of complex-analysis & complex-dynamics visualization tools that share common packages and hand data off to one another.

Dynamics

Complex Dynamics

GPU escape-time visualizer for families f(z, c) — Mandelbrot/multibrot, Julia sets, Tricorn/multicorn, Böttcher coordinates, external rays, matings, deep zoom.

Quadrature

Quadrature Domains

Solver & visualizer for (log-weighted) quadrature domains — inverse and direct problems, single-valued Schwarz-reflection dynamics, limit sets, sphere view, parameter-slice sweeps.

Coming soon

Correspondences

Anti-holomorphic correspondences / Schwarz-reflection mating tool, built on the shared packages: the deltoid Schwarz reflection σ (CPU + GPU), its deleted correspondence, a family parameter plane, a parabolic-Tricorn model space, and an interactive mating visualizer. Built and tested, but not yet part of the published site.

Coming soon

Riemann Map

Research-grade Riemann-map & conformal-mapping studio: custom conformal maps, domain coloring and grid images, the |φ′| distortion field, conformal invariants, and multiple construction methods (Böttcher, lightning/rational, Schwarz–Christoffel, zipper). Scaffolded on the shared packages; not yet part of the published site.

Launcher for the deployed suite: this page sits at the top-level Pages URL and each app is served from a subpath beneath it (e.g. complex-dynamics/). In local development, run an app directly, e.g. pnpm --filter complex-dynamics dev.
