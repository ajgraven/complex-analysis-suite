Complex Analysis Suite

A suite of complex-analysis & complex-dynamics visualization tools that share common packages and hand data off to one another.

Dynamics

Complex Dynamics

GPU escape-time visualizer for families f(z, c) — Mandelbrot/multibrot, Julia sets, Tricorn/multicorn, Böttcher coordinates, external rays, matings, deep zoom.

Quadrature

Quadrature Domains

Solver & visualizer for (log-weighted) quadrature domains — inverse and direct problems, single-valued Schwarz-reflection dynamics, limit sets, sphere view, parameter-slice sweeps.

Conformal maps

Riemann Map

Research-grade Riemann-map & conformal-mapping studio: the image of the unit disk under a conformal map — from the editor, a numerical region map 𝔻 → Ω by the lightning method, or an exterior (Böttcher) map imported from Complex Dynamics — shown pure-2D as the source region and its image in linked canvas panes; plus the numerical Riemann map of a chosen domain.

Plotter

Complex Function Plotting Tool

Research-grade plotter for a single map f(z): domain coloring and enhanced phase portraits, a 3D analytic-landscape surface and Riemann sphere, located zeros & poles, special functions Γ / ζ with honest float32 labeling, hi-res export, and suite interop with Complex Dynamics.

Winding

Argument Principle

An educational visualizer for the argument principle: the winding number of the image curve f(γ) about the origin equals the zeros minus poles of f enclosed by the contour γ. A dual z-plane / w-plane view of a draggable (or freehand) contour and its image, located zeros & poles counted inside γ, an animated traversal, and suite hand-off.

Faber

Faber Transform

A visualizer for the exterior Faber transform Φφ: a function f analytic on the unit disk beside its image on the bounded complement K = ℂ∖Ω of an unbounded domain. Curated univalent domains (interval, ellipse, deltoid, star), exact monomial and pole inputs plus free-form f with an honest convergence equipotential, GPU phase-portrait coloring, the Faber-polynomial roots, and pan/zoom.

Fields & flow

2D Electrostatics

An interactive realization of the complex potential W(z) = φ + iψ: drop and drag charges, sources, sinks, vortices, and doublets and read the field as field lines, equipotentials, streamlines, and a domain-colored field. An Electrostatic ↔ Fluid lens, a flux/circulation probe (the residue theorem as Gauss's law + Kelvin circulation), a draggable sensor puck, animated tracer flow, classic presets (Rankine, cylinder), and permalinks + PNG export. Plus conformal-transplant pages — flow past a Joukowski/Kármán–Trefftz airfoil, and flow past or inside a polygon via Schwarz–Christoffel — and a potential-theory view of a conductor K (its equilibrium charge, capacity, and Green's function; Faber & Fekete points).

Free-boundary flow

Hele-Shaw Flow

Free-boundary flow in a Hele-Shaw cell, as a conformal map of the disk evolving in time: the exact Graven–Makarov “twisting” quadrature domain, driven by a complex charge and grown to a double point or a (3,2)-cusp (drivable by a domain authored in the Quadrature Domains app), and a numerical interior-droplet Polubarinova–Galin evolver — injection smooths, suction fingers into a cusp, with the conserved Richardson moments as the honest error bar.
